1. 🤖 AI Spending Copilot

Instead of only showing charts, let users ask:

"Where did I spend the most this month?"
"Can I afford ₹8,000 this weekend?"
"Why did my expenses increase this month?"
"Find subscriptions I barely use."
"How much can I save if I reduce food spending by 15%?"

Use an LLM to turn transaction data into useful explanations.
